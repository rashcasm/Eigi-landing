What is Eigi Computer?

An AI co-worker with its own computer. It works in your apps around the clock and checks with you before doing anything you haven't approved.

Eigi Computer runs an AI co-worker, called an Eigi, on a computer reserved for your account. That computer stays on, so your Eigi keeps working after you close the dashboard. It answers messages, runs scheduled jobs, and finishes long tasks overnight.

You give it work by messaging it in Slack, WhatsApp, or the dashboard. There's no code to write and no workflow builder. It's built for small teams, usually under ten people, who want an Eigi to cover the ops, support, or technical work they haven't hired for yet.

How an Eigi works

Every Eigi has five parts. Each one has its own page in these docs.

PartWhat it does
PodThe computer your Eigi runs on. It has a browser, files, and a terminal, and it stays on 24/7.
MemoryWhat your Eigi knows about your company, like your customers, prices, and how you want things done. You can read and edit it on the Memory page.
ConnectorsAccess to apps like Gmail, Slack, and Notion. If an app has no connector, your Eigi can use its website in the browser instead.
ChannelsWhere you talk to your Eigi. Every channel shares the same memory, so you can start a task in Slack and check on it from WhatsApp.
PermissionsRules for what your Eigi does on its own, what needs your approval, and what it's never allowed to do.

A task, start to finish

A home-cleaning company uses an Eigi to handle new leads. At 11 p.m., someone fills out the quote form on their website. Here's what happens.

  1. The form triggers an automationA webhook sends the lead's name, phone number, address, and preferred date to the Eigi.
  2. The Eigi checks what it knowsMemory has the service area and price list. The address is in range, so the lead qualifies.
  3. It waits for a reasonable hourThe owner's standing instructions say no calls after 8 p.m., so the Eigi schedules the call for 9 a.m.
  4. It calls the leadAt 9 a.m. it calls through an eigi.ai voice agent, confirms the details, and offers two open slots from the owner's calendar.
  5. It books the job and drafts the quoteThe lead picks Thursday at 2 p.m. The Eigi adds the booking to the calendar and drafts a confirmation email with the price.
  6. It asks before emailing a customerCustomer emails are set to need approval, so the Eigi posts the draft in chat and waits. The owner replies "send" and the email goes out.

The owner can open the session history later and see each of these steps.

Using Eigi with your team

Everyone on your team gets their own Eigis, each with private memory. One person can run several, for example one for sales and one for support.

To let two people's Eigis work together, add each other to your networks and grant access. After that, your Eigi can ask your CTO's Eigi when a fix ships instead of messaging your CTO. Each Eigi shares only what its access allows. Network covers the setup.

What it checks with you first

You choose how independent your Eigi is in Settings → Permissions. In Careful mode, it asks before it sends, deletes, buys, or posts anything. Start there, then let specific tasks run on their own once you trust how your Eigi handles them.

Your Eigi never asks for passwords or one-time codes in chat. It uses a secure prompt instead. Everything it does is recorded in the session history.

You can talk to your Eigi in Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or the dashboard. See Channels to connect one.
